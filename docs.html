Documentation & User Guide

Quick Tip: Make sure Live Activities is enabled in Settings → Caraoke on your iPhone so lyrics can mirror to CarPlay and Lock Screen.

1. CarPlay on iOS 26 Setup

Caraoke mirrors real-time synchronized lyrics to Apple CarPlay using ActivityKit and CarPlay widgets.

  1. Connect your iPhone to your car via wireless or wired CarPlay.
  2. Open Caraoke on your iPhone and toggle Live Lyrics ON.
  3. Start playing music on Spotify or Apple Music.
  4. On your CarPlay screen, swipe to your dashboard widget view to view lyrics alongside maps.

2. Dynamic Island & Lock Screen

On iPhone 14 Pro and later, lyrics appear in the Dynamic Island while playing music.

How to enable:

  1. Toggle Live Lyrics ON in the Caraoke app.
  2. Start playing music.
  3. Lyrics will automatically appear in the Dynamic Island and Lock Screen.

Troubleshooting:

If lyrics don't appear, try:

  • Toggle Live Lyrics OFF and ON again.
  • Restart the Caraoke app.
  • Make sure Live Activities are enabled in iOS Settings → Caraoke.

3. Home Screen Widgets

Caraoke offers three widget sizes: Small, Medium, and Large.

How to add widgets:

  1. Long-press on your Home Screen.
  2. Tap the + button in the top-left corner.
  3. Search for Caraoke.
  4. Choose your preferred widget size and tap Add Widget.

Widget Features:

  • Vinyl Animation: The vinyl disc rotates while music plays.
  • Tap to Refresh: Tap the vinyl or album art to manually refresh lyrics.
  • Themes: Choose from Pitch Black, Slate, or Ivory White in Widget Settings.
  • StandBy Mode: Place your iPhone horizontally on a charger for full-screen lyrics.

4. Troubleshooting & Common Problems

Lyrics not updating or showing "Paused"

Ensure that either Apple Music or Spotify is actively selected as the source. If using Spotify, ensure the app has completed Spotify authorization.

Widget not updating automatically

iOS limits widget update frequency. To force an update:

  • Tap the vinyl disc or album artwork in the widget.
  • Enable Live Activities for real-time updates.

Missing lyrics for some songs

Caraoke uses multiple lyrics providers with automatic fallback. If a song doesn't have lyrics:

  • We search LRCLIB first (primary source).
  • If not found, we search NetEase (secondary source).
  • If still not found, the song may not have synced lyrics available.

Spotify connection issues

If Spotify disconnects frequently:

  1. Go to Settings → Music Sources → Spotify Setup.
  2. Try re-authorizing Spotify.
  3. Consider using a Custom Client ID (see section 5).

5. Configuring a Custom Spotify Client ID

Power users who wish to use their own Spotify developer credentials can enter their Custom Client ID:

  1. Go to Spotify Developer Dashboard.
  2. Create a new app and copy the Client ID.
  3. In Caraoke, go to Settings → Music Sources → Spotify Setup → Advanced.
  4. Enter your Custom Client ID and save.

6. StandBy Mode

Place your iPhone horizontally on a MagSafe charger to enjoy full-screen lyrics on your nightstand or desk.

How to use:

  1. Add the Caraoke widget to your StandBy screen.
  2. Place your iPhone horizontally on a charger.
  3. Start playing music.
  4. Lyrics will appear full-screen.

The widget supports Pitch Black, Simple Slate, and Ivory White themes. Change themes in Widget Settings.
