1. The service

Caraoke (“the App”) displays time-synced song lyrics on your iPhone and on surfaces iOS makes available to Live Activities (including CarPlay). It is a lyrics display companion, not a music service. It reads playback from Apple Music or Spotify, may send playback control commands to Spotify when authorized, caches lyric text locally, and sends the active track's lyric schedule to a relay so Live Activities can continue while the app is suspended. It does not sell music, provide a downloadable lyric catalog, or distribute audio.

Lyric text may be copyrighted by its respective rights holders.

If you are a rights holder and believe lyrics displayed through the App infringe your rights, contact support@caraoke.app. Removal or source changes are handled case by case; no particular response time or outcome is promised.

Caraoke is intended for use while parked or by passengers.
