Third parties

  • LRCLIB (lrclib.net) and YouTube Music (InnerTube): Caraoke races both providers when matching a song. Requests include the track title and artist; LRCLIB also receives duration. No Caraoke account is used. Their own terms and privacy policies apply.
  • Caraoke lyrics relay (Cloudflare): receives the Live Activity push token, track title/artist, full lyric schedule, start/end times, and playing state. One Durable Object is used per device, keyed by the push token. The relay also stores crash payloads (currently the most recent 20 records). Security review and deployment of access controls for these records are unresolved; do not treat crash reports as private or access-controlled.
  • Sentry: receives crash and non-fatal error diagnostics, including stack traces and device/app metadata. Automatic session tracking and watchdog-termination tracking are enabled.
  • TelemetryDeck: receives the usage signals listed above. It is designed for privacy-friendly analytics, but it is still a third-party analytics service.
  • RevenueCat: receives purchase and subscription records and entitlement state, linked to the app user identifier RevenueCat uses. Apple StoreKit remains the transaction source.
  • Spotify: if you connect it, Caraoke requests user-read-currently-playing, user-read-playback-state, and user-modify-playback-state. This means Caraoke can read playback state and send playback control commands. Disconnecting removes the local token; it does not revoke the authorization grant at Spotify. Revoke access in your Spotify account settings.
  • Apple: Apple Music playback is read through MediaPlayer; purchases are processed by Apple.

Retention and deletion

  • Cached lyric text remains in the app's Caches folder until iOS or an app update removes it; there is currently no in-app control to clear it. Disconnecting Spotify removes the local token from the shared Keychain but does not revoke the authorization grant at Spotify; revoke access in your Spotify account settings.
  • Stopping a ride deletes the relay session only if the end request reaches the relay. If the app goes offline before that request succeeds, the session may remain until it expires. The most recent 20 crash records may remain in relay storage until cleared; no deletion timeline is guaranteed. Sentry, TelemetryDeck, RevenueCat, and Cloudflare retain data under their own policies.
  • Uninstalling the app removes local app data, but it is not a guaranteed deletion of records already held by the relay, Sentry, TelemetryDeck, RevenueCat, Cloudflare, or Apple. Contact support@caraoke.app to request deletion; deletion of server-side records is not guaranteed.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no sale of personal data
  • No Caraoke accounts and no listening-history profile
  • No promise that data is anonymous; the app uses third-party services listed above
  • No promise that crash reports are private or access-controlled

Your controls

There is currently no in-app control to clear cached lyrics; uninstalling the app removes local app data. Disconnecting Spotify removes the local token but does not revoke access at Spotify — revoke it in your Spotify account settings. Stop Live Lyrics to end the relay session. For other requests, contact support.

Children: Caraoke is not designed for children and offers no accounts. If you believe a child's information was submitted, contact support.
