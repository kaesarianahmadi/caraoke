1. CarPlay setup

Caraoke mirrors synchronized lyrics to CarPlay using ActivityKit and the CarPlay widget tile.

  1. Connect your iPhone to your car via wireless or wired CarPlay.
  2. On iPhone, open Caraoke → Settings (gear on Home) → Connect music. Choose Apple Music, or connect Spotify.
  3. On Home, switch Live Lyrics on.
  4. Allow Live Activities: iOS Settings → Caraoke → Live Activities.
  5. Start playing music, then open the CarPlay dashboard to see lyrics alongside maps.

Nothing needs configuring while driving. On touchscreen CarPlay, the lyrics and hybrid tiles show a centered Resync arrow for a known expired timeline; the hybrid and player covers can also request resync. Playback controls are available on the player and hybrid tiles.

2. Lock Screen & Dynamic Island

On iPhone 14 Pro and later, lyrics can also appear in the Dynamic Island while music plays.

Enable

  1. Open Caraoke → Settings (gear on Home) → Connect music and choose your music source.
  2. On Home, switch Live Lyrics on.
  3. Allow Live Activities: iOS Settings → Caraoke → Live Activities.
  4. Start playing music. Lyrics appear on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island when iOS delivers the Live Activity.

If lyrics don't appear

  • Confirm Live Activities are enabled in iOS Settings → Caraoke → Live Activities.
  • Toggle Live Lyrics off and on again.
  • Open Caraoke once so it can re-read the player and re-arm the current track.

3. Home Screen widgets

Caraoke offers Small, Medium, and Large widgets.

Add a widget

  1. Long-press the Home Screen.
  2. Tap + in the top-left corner.
  3. Search for Caraoke.
  4. Pick a size and tap Add Widget.

Widget features

  • Vinyl animation: the disc rotates while music plays.
  • Tap to refresh: tap the vinyl or album art to fetch lyrics immediately.
  • Themes: Pitch Black, Slate, or Ivory White in widget settings.
  • StandBy: place the iPhone horizontally on a charger for full-screen lyrics.

4. StandBy mode

Place your iPhone horizontally on a MagSafe charger for full-screen lyrics on a nightstand or desk.

  1. Add the Caraoke widget to your StandBy screen.
  2. Place the iPhone horizontally on a charger.
  3. Start playing music — lyrics appear full-screen.

5. Locked phone & background limits

iOS suspends Caraoke about 30 seconds after you leave the app. To keep the current song's lines moving while the phone is locked, Caraoke hands that song's lyric timeline to a push relay before it is suspended.

One song at a time — not continuous background sync. The relay is armed only for the track that was playing. If you change track, pause, or seek while Caraoke is suspended, the tile can hold the previous line or keep counting until you tap Resync (immediate sample and re-arm) or reopen Caraoke (normal polling resumes, but no immediate sample or re-arm). Background delivery is not guaranteed.

  • Track change, pause, or seek while suspended: tap Resync (arrow.clockwise) or the album art / vinyl disc on the widget or touchscreen CarPlay tile, or tap Resync in the phone Lyrics page header. Opening Caraoke only resumes normal polling.
  • Activity lifetime: iOS ends a Live Activity after roughly 8 hours. Reopen Caraoke to start a fresh one.
  • Delays: Low Power Mode, Focus, or Live Activities being off can delay or hide updates.
  • While unlocked: with Caraoke open, it polls the player about once a second and re-aligns within a couple of seconds after a seek.

6. Troubleshooting

Lyrics not updating, or stuck on "Paused"

Make sure the active source is the app that is actually playing. Open Caraoke → Settings (gear on Home) → Connect music and select Apple Music or Spotify. For Spotify, confirm the row shows connected; if not, expand the Spotify row and choose Reconnect.

Lyrics freeze or drift after locking the phone

  1. Tap Resync in the phone Lyrics page header, or the centered arrow on a Caraoke CarPlay lyrics/hybrid tile when it appears for a known expired timeline. Album art and vinyl also request Resync. The arrow does not detect every timing error; when Caraoke handles a tap, it samples the selected player. The widget-extension fallback repairs Spotify only — Apple Music needs the app path.
  2. Or open Caraoke from the widget. Normal polling resumes, but opening the app does not force an immediate sample or relay re-arm — use Resync for that.
  3. Still stale? Switch Live Lyrics off, then on.
  4. Confirm Settings → Connect music matches the app that is actually playing.
  5. Paused or seeked from the car? Resume playback, then seek once in the track. With Caraoke open, it re-aligns within a couple of seconds.
  6. No tile at all? Check iOS Settings → Caraoke → Live Activities, then switch Live Lyrics off and on.
  7. The Lock Screen and Dynamic Island have no Resync control — open Caraoke or use the phone Lyrics page header instead.

Widget not updating automatically

iOS chooses when widgets refresh on its own; continuous background updates are not guaranteed. To force an update, tap the vinyl disc, album art, or Resync in the widget, or use Live Lyrics for line-by-line updates while the activity is delivered.

Missing lyrics for some songs

Caraoke races LRCLIB and YouTube Music (InnerTube) at the same time and uses the first valid synchronized lyric sheet that comes back. There is no NetEase fallback.

If neither provider returns synchronized lyrics, the song plays without lines — the title, artist, and progress still update.

Spotify keeps disconnecting

  1. Open Caraoke → Settings (gear on Home) → Connect music, then expand the Spotify row.
  2. Choose Reconnect to re-authorize.
  3. If it persists, use your own free Client ID (section 7).

Lyrics feel out of sync

Seek within the track once; with Caraoke open, it re-aligns to the playback position reported by the music source. Bluetooth latency in some cars can shift the perceived timing by a fraction of a second.

7. Custom Spotify Client ID

Spotify support is a limited beta. The built-in shared credentials are tied to a Spotify development-mode app, which Spotify limits to a 5-user allowlist. If you see an authorization error or HTTP 403, connect with your own free Client ID — the app owner is exempt from that allowlist.

  1. Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard and log in.
  2. Create an app named Caraoke and select Web API.
  3. Under App Settings → Redirect URIs, add exactly caraoke://callback.
  4. Copy the Client ID from Basic Information.
  5. In Caraoke: Settings (gear on Home) → Music Sources → tap Spotify → paste the Client ID → Save Custom ID & Connect.

To repair a broken connection, reopen the Spotify row and choose Reconnect. Disconnect is in the same expanded panel.

9. Collecting logs for support

For timing or refresh bugs, a fresh device log helps more than a screenshot. Use a new, uniquely named archive for every report so an older capture is never mistaken for the current one.

With a Mac and a USB cable

  1. Connect the iPhone by USB, unlock it, and tap Trust if prompted.
  2. Open Console.app on the Mac, select the iPhone in the sidebar, and click Start streaming.
  3. Filter by process Caraoke (add CaraokeWidgets for widget issues), reproduce the problem, then save the session.

Or from Terminal, name the archive with the build, date, and time:

sudo log collect --device-name "Your iPhone" --last 10m \
  --output ~/Desktop/caraoke-build84-20261114-0135.logarchive

Replace the build number and timestamp with the build under test and the current local time. Do not reuse or rename an older archive — collect a fresh one while the issue is visible.

Logs can contain device and app activity, so review the archive before sending it. Also include the TestFlight or App Store build number, iOS version, iPhone model, CarPlay connection type (wired or wireless), and music source (Spotify or Apple Music).
