Documentation & User Guide

Quick Tip: Make sure Live Activities is enabled in Settings → Caraoke on your iPhone so lyrics can mirror to CarPlay.

1. CarPlay on iOS 26 Setup

Caraoke mirrors real-time synchronized lyrics to Apple CarPlay using ActivityKit and supplemental widgets.

  1. Connect your iPhone to your car via wireless or wired CarPlay.
  2. Open Caraoke on your iPhone and toggle Live Lyrics ON.
  3. Start playing music on Spotify or Apple Music.
  4. On your CarPlay screen, swipe to your dashboard widget view to view lyrics alongside maps.

2. Troubleshooting & Common Problems

Lyrics not updating or showing "Paused"

Ensure that either Apple Music or Spotify is actively selected as the source. If using Spotify, ensure the app has completed Spotify authorization.

Tapping album artwork in widgets

Tapping the vinyl disc or album artwork in your desktop or Lock Screen widget fires an instant resync request to refresh the timeline.

Missing lyrics for obscure or new songs

Caraoke features a multi-source provider fallback engine: it first searches LRCLIB, and if unavailable, automatically queries secondary open lyric databases.

3. Configuring a Custom Spotify Client ID

Power users who wish to use their own Spotify developer credentials can enter their Custom Client ID in Settings → Music Sources → Spotify Setup → Advanced.

4. StandBy Mode

Place your iPhone horizontally on a MagSafe charger to enjoy full-screen lyrics on your nightstand or desk. The widget supports Pitch Black, Simple Slate, and Ivory White themes.
